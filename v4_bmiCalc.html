Important Warnings for a BMI App

BMI App's Disclaimers:

  • BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.
  • People with high muscle mass (e.g., athletes) may have a high BMI without excess body fat.
  • BMI categories may not apply equally to children, adolescents, pregnant individuals, or some older adults.
  • If you have symptoms such as unexplained weight loss or gain, or other health concerns, consult a healthcare professional regardless of your BMI.
